# Time Symmetry and Entropy in AP Physics

This AP Research project explores how AP Physics materials explain microscopic time-reversal symmetry and macroscopic irreversibility, and what conceptual gaps students report.

The study analyzes four AP-aligned materials and an anonymous survey of five AP Physics students. Its findings are exploratory and limited by the small sample.
